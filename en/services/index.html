Services

Palvast takes the administration of pooled pallets off your hands. We become part of your workflow: we manage part of it and advise on part of it.

The rhythm

Weekly

We process the movement data and make the declarations to the pooler.

Monthly

We check the invoices.

Yearly

We carry out the inventory validation on your behalf.

Insight into your way of working

We review your internal way of working and set up data collection, including inbound movements. That way we flag mismatches against the pooler's bookings early.

Own platform, expertise at the core

Our software with normalized movement data enables the service. The core of the service is pooling expertise.
